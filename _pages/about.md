I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Engineering at [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/), Germany, working in the [EclectX research group](https://eclectx.org/) under the supervision of Prof. Nima TaheriNejad at the Institute of Computer Engineering (ZITI). My research lies at the intersection of emerging computing technologies, digital circuit design, and computer architecture.

I am particularly interested in **memristive in-memory computing**, **emerging computing paradigms**, and **cross-layer hardware design**. My research explores how emerging non-volatile memory technologies can enable more efficient and reliable computing systems by integrating computation directly into memory. A central focus of my work is the development of **novel circuits and systems, computing architectures, and open-source electronic design automation (EDA) tools**. I am particularly interested in bridging different levels of the computing stack, from device-level characteristics and circuit design to alternative computing paradigms, such as approximate and stochastic computing, and system-level applications in image processing, machine learning, and signal processing.

Before joining Heidelberg University, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (2023) and my M.Sc. in Embedded Systems (2025), both from [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/), Austria. During my master's studies, I worked on the OCTAVE project under Prof. Axel Jantsch, focusing on edge AI and computer vision for railway applications. In parallel, I conducted research on approximate arithmetic for memristive in-memory computing under the supervision of Prof. Nima TaheriNejad.
