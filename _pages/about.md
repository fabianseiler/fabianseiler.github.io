I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Engineering at **Heidelberg University, Germany**, where I am part of the **EclectX research group**.

My research focuses on **emerging computing technologies**, particularly memristive in-memory computing, digital stateful logic, and approximate computing. I am interested in developing efficient and reliable computing architectures and design methodologies that bridge the gap between emerging device technologies and practical computing applications.

A central focus of my work is the development of **device-aware algorithms and open-source electronic design automation (EDA) tools** for memristive computing, spanning device-level characterization, circuit design, logic synthesis, and system-level evaluation.

## Background

I received my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (2023) and my M.Sc. in Embedded Systems (2025), both from **TU Wien, Austria**.

I have authored more than 10 scientific publications and served as a reviewer for several academic journals. My work has been recognized with multiple merit scholarships, including the Palfinger ELEVATE Scholarship, and I won the **IEEE SMACD 2025 EDA Competition**.
