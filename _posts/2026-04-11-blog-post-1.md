Welcome to my personal website and research blog!

I am a Ph.D. researcher at Heidelberg University, working in the [EclectX research group](https://eclectx.org/) under the supervision of Prof. Nima TaheriNejad.

I created this website to share my research, publications, projects, and experiences throughout my academic journey.

## What Am I Working On?

My research focuses on **emerging computing technologies**, particularly memristive in-memory computing, digital stateful logic, and approximate computing. I am interested in developing efficient computing architectures and open-source EDA tools that bridge emerging device technologies and practical applications.

## What to Expect

Through this blog, I plan to share **research updates, technical insights, open-source projects, and academic experiences**. This includes new publications, interesting findings, lessons learned, and ideas worth exploring.
